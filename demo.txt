3️⃣ INTERVIEW Q&A (REAL QUESTIONS, REAL ANSWERS)
Q1: Is this a product or a service?

Answer:
This is an internal data platform service. It’s not a customer-facing product, but a production system used by internal teams to power analytics and decision-making.

Q2: Who are the end users?

Answer:
The direct users are data engineers and analysts. Business users consume the output indirectly through dashboards built in Superset.

Q3: How do you handle failures?

Answer:
Failures are isolated per source. We retry transient errors, alert via Slack, and optionally create incidents for tracking. One failing source does not block others.

Q4: How is data quality enforced?

Answer:
We run configurable quality checks after ingestion, supporting soft and hard failures. This allows us to protect downstream analytics without unnecessarily blocking ingestion.

Q5: How do you support backfill?

Answer:
Backfill is configuration-driven. The same ingestion logic is reused for historical dates, ensuring consistency and avoiding duplication.

Q6: How do you make it production-ready?

Answer:
By combining CI/CD, secure credential handling, observability, runbooks, and analytics integration. Production is about operations, not just code.

Q7: Why Airflow?

Answer:
Airflow provides reliable scheduling, retries, visibility, and operational control, which are critical for long-running data platforms.
